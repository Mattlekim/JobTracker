Your round, in your pocket

Work Tracker runs a window-cleaning round from one app: who you clean for, what is due, what has been paid, what it all cost — and the figures the taxman wants at the end of the year.

No subscriptions. No account to sign up for. Your round stays on your phone.

One app for the whole round

A round is more than a list of jobs. It is the sheet you take out in the morning, the days you have promised people, the money still owed, the fuel receipts in the glovebox and the tax return in April. Work Tracker keeps all of it in one place, and each part is explained on its own page:

Work

The round five ways: the paper sheet you work from, the list of what is due, every house you have, the quotes you have priced up, and the figures for the round as a whole.

Made for how the work actually happens

Works with wet hands Swipe a job done, skip a house with nobody in, and let the tag bar mark the whole day "front only" so you are not typing at a gate.
Rounds, not just jobs Split the work into rounds — a village, a day of the week — and see each round's houses, hours and value on its own.
Counts like you do Two visits due at one house is two jobs on the list but one house on the round. The figures are worked out the way a window cleaner counts.
Nothing sent anywhere No cloud account is needed. Your customers' names and addresses stay on your phone unless you back them up yourself.
